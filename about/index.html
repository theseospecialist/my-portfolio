What Scuba Diving Taught Me About SEO

I'm a certified advanced open water diver, and the habits that keep divers safe are the same ones I use on every SEO project: plan first, watch the data, look below the surface and never take shortcuts.

  • Plan the Dive, Dive the PlanDivers agree the route before entering the water. Every project starts with an audit and a written plan before anything changes on your site.
  • Watch Your GaugesDivers check air and depth constantly. I check Search Console, GA4 and rankings the same way, so problems are caught early, not after a month of lost leads.
  • Look Below the SurfaceA traffic drop is what you see. The cause is usually underneath: a canonical tag, a noindex, a slow server or a blocked crawler.
  • No Fast AscentsRushing to the surface is how divers get hurt. Bought links and spun content are how sites get penalized. Steady, safe growth lasts.
  • Never Dive AloneDivers always have a buddy. You work directly with me, and I report every change, so you always know what is happening on your site.
