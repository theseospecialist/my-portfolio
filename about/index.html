About Salman Yousufzai

I'm a senior SEO specialist based in Karachi, Pakistan, with 5 years in SEO and 8 in digital marketing. I help businesses get found on Google and in AI answers, and I do the work myself.

My Story

I started in digital marketing in 2018, running social media and paid campaigns. Search pulled me in because it rewards real work: fix the right things, publish the right content, and the numbers move.

Today I'm Senior SEO Executive at Carpe Diem Team, leading SEO for six client accounts and five Google Business Profile locations across hospitality, home services, AV integration and marine retail. Before that, at Sybex Lab, I earned top-3 rankings for high-value terms and led a 6-person SEO team.

I'm also a certified advanced open water diver. It shapes how I work: most SEO stops at the surface, and the results are usually further down.
