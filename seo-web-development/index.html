Why SEO Belongs in the Build, Not After It

Many websites look good on launch day and then struggle in search. Templates load heavy scripts, important text only appears after JavaScript runs, headings are chosen for looks rather than meaning, and a redesign quietly breaks old URLs.

Fixing those problems later costs more than building them right. When SEO is part of the build, every template has one clear H1, a self-referencing canonical, structured data and fast-loading images from the start.

  3. 03

    Validate Structured Data

    Every schema type tested so it is eligible and error-free.

  4. 04

    Map Every Old URL

    For redesigns, each old address gets a 301 redirect to the right new page.

  5. 05

    Remove Staging Blocks

    Noindex tags and robots.txt blocks from development are removed at launch, a common cause of traffic loss.

    Submit and Monitor

    The sitemap is submitted in Search Console and indexing is watched for the first weeks.

SEO Web Development Questions

Will a New Website Improve My Rankings?

A faster, better structured site removes technical barriers and makes good content easier to rank, but rankings also depend on content and authority. A redesign done carelessly can lose rankings, which is why redirects and checks matter.

Which Platform Should I Use?

It depends on your business. WordPress suits most service businesses, Shopify suits online stores, GoHighLevel suits teams that live in its CRM, and hand-coded HTML gives the best speed for simple sites.

Can You Redesign My Site Without Losing Rankings?

That's the goal of a proper migration: every old URL mapped to a new one with a 301 redirect, content kept or improved, and Search Console monitored after launch.

Do You Also Write the Content?

Yes. I can write the service pages and content, so the site launches with pages built to rank rather than placeholder text.

How Fast Will My New Site Be?

I build against Google's Core Web Vitals thresholds. This site scores 100 on desktop and 97 on mobile in PageSpeed Insights; your score depends on your content, images and platform.
