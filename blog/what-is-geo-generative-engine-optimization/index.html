Quick answer: GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work of making your content easy for AI search tools such as Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity to find, understand and cite. It builds on SEO: the page must be indexed and trusted, then structured so a clear answer can be lifted and credited to you.

What GEO Means

When someone asks Google a question, an AI-written summary often appears above the regular results. When someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity, the answer comes with a handful of source links. GEO is about becoming one of those sources.

You'll also hear two related terms. Here's how they fit together:

TermGoalWhere You Show Up
SEORank your pages in search resultsThe organic results on Google and Bing
AEOGive the direct answer to a questionFeatured snippets, People Also Ask, voice answers
GEOBe quoted inside AI-written answersAI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity

They aren't separate projects. Good SEO is the foundation, AEO sharpens your answers, and GEO makes sure AI tools can use them.

Why GEO Matters for Your Business

AI answers sit at the very top of the page, above the first organic result. Being cited there puts your brand in front of the searcher before they see any competitor, even if they don't click straight away.

It is measurable, too. For a home improvement brand, the content I planned and published earned 12.2K impressions inside Google's AI Overviews in three months, tracked in Search Console. A single guide was responsible for 2,714 of them.

How AI Tools Choose Which Sources to Cite

No AI company publishes its full formula. But from Google's own guidance and from tracking what gets cited, a few patterns are clear:

  1. IndexedPages already in the index and trusted
  2. DirectA clear answer near the top
  3. StructuredHeadings, lists and tables
  4. SpecificReal numbers, places and steps
  5. KnownConsistent details about who you are

7 Steps to Get Your Business Cited in AI Answers

  1. 01

    Get the SEO Basics Right First

    If a page has a noindex tag, a missing title, no H1 or blocks crawlers, AI tools can't use it. Run my free SEO checker to catch the basics in under a minute.

  2. 02

    Answer the Question in the First Two Sentences

    Open each page or section with the answer, then explain. AI systems lift short, self-contained statements most easily.

  3. 03

    Write Headings That Match Real Questions

    Use the words people actually search, such as How Much Does Local SEO Cost, so each section clearly answers one question.

  4. 04

    Add FAQs and Structured Data

    A short FAQ answers follow-up questions, and schema markup states your business details explicitly.

  5. 05

    Be Specific

    Include price ranges, timeframes, locations, steps and real results. Specifics are what a summary needs and what generic content lacks.

  6. 06

    Keep Your Business Details Consistent Everywhere

    Your name, location, services and profiles should match across your site, your Business Profile and directories, so AI tools know which business you are.

  7. 07

    Track Your AI Visibility

    Watch Search Console, which includes AI Overview traffic in its Performance report, and check how assistants answer questions about your services each month.

What About llms.txt?

An llms.txt file is a plain-text summary of your site written for AI tools. It is quick to add and does no harm, but it is not a ranking factor and no major search engine requires it. This site has one if you want an example. Focus on the steps above first.

Common GEO Mistakes

  • Skipping SEO and jumping to AI tricks. There's no shortcut around being indexable, fast and trusted.
  • Burying the answer. Long introductions push the useful sentence too far down.
  • Writing for algorithms instead of people. Stuffed keywords read badly to humans and AI alike.
  • Thin pages. A 150-word service page gives an AI tool nothing to quote.
  • Not measuring. Without tracking, you can't tell what's working.
GEO Checklist

The page is indexable and fast, the first two sentences answer the main question, headings use the words customers search, there's a short FAQ with structured data, the page includes specific numbers and steps, business details match across the web, and AI visibility is checked monthly.

Questions Answered

Is GEO Replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. AI tools mostly cite pages that are already indexed, trusted and well structured, so strong SEO is still the foundation.

How Long Does GEO Take to Work?

Once a page is indexed and ranking, clearer answers and structure can earn citations within weeks. New sites usually need a few months of SEO first.

Can Small Businesses Do GEO?

Yes. Local businesses often have an advantage, because they can answer specific questions about their services and area better than national sites.

Does Schema Guarantee My Content Will Be Cited?

No. Structured data helps search engines understand your page, but no markup guarantees a citation. Clear, useful content matters most.
