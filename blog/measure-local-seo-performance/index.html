Quick answer: Measure local SEO with customer actions first: calls, direction requests and website clicks from your Google Business Profile, then organic clicks and leads from your website in Search Console and Analytics. Track the same metrics every month, compare with the same period last year, and connect them to real enquiries.

Measure Actions, Not Just Rankings

Rankings change by location, device and even the time of day, so a single ranking report can mislead. Customer actions don't lie: a call, a direction request or a form submission is a real person choosing your business.

Compare Like With Like

Local businesses are seasonal. A restaurant's summer will always look different from its winter. Compare each month with the same month last year where you can. Google's Business Profile panels show year-over-year changes automatically, which is how I can report figures like +600.1% direction requests for a Florida restaurant location with confidence.

Measurement Mistakes to Avoid

  • Reporting only rankings. They vary by location and don't pay the bills.
  • Comparing different seasons. Use year-over-year comparisons where you have the data.
  • Ignoring lead quality. Ten calls from your service area beat fifty from outside it.
  • Changing many things at once with no notes. Keep a simple log of changes so you can connect results to actions.

Common Questions

What Is the Most Important Local SEO Metric?

The number of real enquiries: calls, direction requests and form submissions from local search. Everything else explains why that number is moving.

How Long Should I Wait Before Judging Local SEO Results?

Give it at least three months for most changes, and compare with the same period last year to account for seasonality.

Why Do My Rankings Look Different on My Phone?

Local rankings depend on where the searcher is. Your phone shows results for your location, which may differ from what customers across town see.

Can I Track Calls From My Business Profile?

Yes. The profile's Performance section shows calls made from the call button. A call tracking number can add detail, but keep your main number consistent across listings.
