How Google AI Overviews Affect Organic Search

By Salman Yousufzai, Senior SEO Specialist · October 31, 2026 · 4 min read

AT A GLANCE
  • Launched in the USMay 2024
  • Shows onMany question-style searches
  • Main effectFewer clicks on simple answers
  • OpportunityBeing cited as a source

Quick answer: Google AI Overviews place an AI-written summary above the regular results for many searches. They can reduce clicks on simple informational questions, because the answer appears on the page, but they also link to the sources they draw from. Pages that are indexed, trusted and answer clearly can earn visibility as one of those cited sources.

What AI Overviews Are

AI Overviews are summaries generated by Google's AI that appear at the top of some search results, with links to the web pages they draw from. Google rolled them out to everyone in the United States in May 2024 and has since expanded them to many more countries and languages. In 2025 Google added AI Mode, a separate, conversational search experience built on the same approach.

  1. SearchA person asks a question
  2. RetrieveGoogle selects relevant indexed pages
  3. SummarizeAI writes an overview
  4. CiteSource links appear alongside
  5. ClickSome users visit a source

Which Searches Show AI Overviews

AI Overviews appear most often on informational searches: questions, comparisons and "how to" queries. They appear less often on navigational searches for a specific brand, and on many local searches where the map pack is the main result.

What Happens to Organic Traffic

When an AI Overview fully answers a simple question, fewer people need to click. Sites that relied on short answers to basic questions feel this most. A common pattern in Search Console is impressions rising while click-through rate falls, because pages appear for more searches but get fewer clicks on some of them.

The other side is visibility inside the answer itself. For a home improvement brand in Manhattan, Search Console showed 12.2K impressions inside AI Overviews in three months, with one guide earning 2,714 of them on its own.

How to Measure the Impact on Your Site

  1. 01

    Check Search Console's Performance Report

    Google includes traffic from AI Overviews and AI Mode in the Performance report under the Web search type. Watch clicks, impressions and CTR for your most important queries over time.

  2. 02

    Compare CTR for Question Queries

    Filter for queries that start with what, how, why or best. If impressions are steady but CTR is falling, AI answers may be satisfying more of those searches.

  3. 03

    Look at the Results Yourself

    Search your top queries in an incognito window and note whether an AI Overview appears and which sources it cites.

  4. 04

    Track Conversions, Not Just Clicks

    Visitors who click through after reading a summary are often further along. Watch leads from organic search alongside traffic.

How to Adapt Your SEO

Google says there is nothing special required to appear in AI Overviews beyond normal SEO best practices: the page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet. In practice, the pages that get cited tend to share a few traits.

  • They answer early. The first sentence under each heading states the answer plainly.
  • They go deeper than the summary. Tables, steps, examples and real numbers give people a reason to click.
  • They are specific. Prices, locations, timeframes and named methods beat generic statements.
  • They show who wrote them. Author details, real experience and sources build trust.
  • They are technically sound. Indexable, fast and well structured, with no snippet restrictions.
Shift Your Goals for Simple Questions

For basic questions, aim to be the cited source and build brand recognition. Put your conversion effort into the deeper guides and service pages people still click through to.

Questions Answered

Can I Stop My Site From Appearing in AI Overviews?

You can limit what Google shows with nosnippet or max-snippet controls, but these also limit how your page appears in regular results. For most businesses, the visibility is worth more than the restriction.

Do AI Overviews Show in Search Console?

Traffic from AI Overviews and AI Mode is included in Search Console's Performance report under the Web search type. It is not reported as a separate standard filter for every site.

Do I Need Special Schema for AI Overviews?

No. Google says no special markup is required. Structured data still helps search engines understand your page, but it does not guarantee inclusion.

Will AI Overviews Replace Organic Search?

They change it rather than replace it. Searchers still click through for detail, comparison, local options and buying decisions.
