Google Business Profile Optimization Best Practices

By Salman Yousufzai, Senior SEO Specialist · October 31, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer: To optimize a Google Business Profile, use your real business name, choose the most accurate primary category, add every service and accurate hours, write a clear 750-character description, upload real photos regularly, post updates, ask every customer for a review and reply to all reviews. Keep everything consistent with your website.

Why Your Business Profile Matters So Much

For local searches, your Business Profile is often the first thing a customer sees, before your website. It shows your rating, photos, hours and a call button. Many customers act straight from the profile without visiting your site at all.

12 Google Business Profile Best Practices

1. Use Your Real Name

Adding city names or keywords to your business name breaks Google's guidelines and risks suspension.

2. Pick the Right Primary Category

It is one of the strongest relevance signals. Look at the categories competitors ranking in your map pack use.

3. Add Every Service

Services help Google match your profile to specific searches, not just your main category.

4. Write a Useful Description

Explain what you do, who you serve and where, in plain language. No links or promotional claims.

5. Keep Hours Accurate

Wrong hours cost visits and reviews. Update holiday hours before every holiday.

6. Upload Real Photos Often

Real work, real team, real location. Add new photos every month rather than all at once.

7. Post Updates Regularly

Offers, events and recent jobs keep the profile active and give customers a reason to choose you.

8. Ask Every Customer for a Review

Use your direct review link. A steady flow of recent reviews matters more than an old total.

9. Reply to Every Review

Thank good reviews and answer bad ones calmly and specifically. Future customers read your replies.

10. Match Your Website

Name, address, phone and hours should match your site and LocalBusiness schema exactly.

11. Add Products or Menus

Restaurants, retailers and service businesses can show menus or products directly on the profile.

12. Check Performance Monthly

Calls, direction requests and website clicks show whether your changes are working.

How to Get More Reviews Without Breaking the Rules

Ask at the moment the customer is happiest: right after the job is finished or the meal is served. Send your direct review link by text or email, and keep the request short.

What Google Does Not Allow

Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews, do not ask only happy customers (review gating), and never post reviews for your own business. These can lead to removed reviews or a suspended profile.

Managing Multiple Locations

Each location needs its own verified profile with its own address, phone number, hours, photos and reviews. For two-location coffee and restaurant brands I manage, each profile gets its own posts and its own monthly report, because performance often differs a lot between locations.

Mistakes That Get Profiles Suspended

  • Keyword stuffing in the business name
  • Using a virtual office or PO box as a storefront address
  • Creating more than one profile for the same location
  • Showing a storefront address when you only serve customers at their locations

Common Questions

How Often Should I Update My Google Business Profile?

Check it at least monthly: add photos, publish a post, reply to new reviews and confirm hours. Update holiday hours before each holiday.

Do Google Business Profile Posts Help Rankings?

Posts mainly help customers choose you and keep the profile active. Treat them as a conversion tool rather than a ranking trick.

Can I Change My Primary Category?

Yes, but choose carefully. Changing it can shift which searches you appear for, sometimes for the worse. Test against what competitors in your map pack use.

What Should I Do About a Fake Negative Review?

Reply calmly and factually, then report the review through your Business Profile if it breaks Google's policies. Google decides whether to remove it.
