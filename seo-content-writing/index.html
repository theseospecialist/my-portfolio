Why Most Business Content Doesn't Rank

Most business blogs are written without a plan. Posts target words nobody searches, two posts compete for the same keyword, and almost none of them link to the pages that actually sell. The result is a lot of writing and very little traffic.

SEO content starts the other way round: with the searches you want to win and the page that should win each one. Then it is written for the person searching, with the answer up front, real detail and a clear next step.

For a home improvement brand in Manhattan, a planned blog program of 102 posts took the site from zero search visibility to 326K impressions, including 12.2K inside Google's AI Overviews.

SEO Content Writing Questions

How Many Blog Posts Do I Need?

Fewer than most people think. Two to four strong posts a month, each targeting a real search and linking to a service page, usually beats a high volume of thin posts.

Do You Use AI to Write Content?

I use tools for research, but every piece is planned, written and checked by me, with real examples and data from your business. Google rewards helpful content, not how it was produced, and generic AI text rarely ranks.

Can You Write for My Industry?

Yes. I have written for home improvement, restaurants, coffee, marine retail, smart home and technology businesses. You provide the expertise; I turn it into content that ranks.

Will You Publish the Content Too?

Yes. I can publish directly in WordPress, Shopify or GoHighLevel, with formatting, images, internal links and schema done.

How Long Before Content Brings Traffic?

New content on an established site can rank within weeks. On newer sites it usually takes a few months, and results compound as more pages are published.
