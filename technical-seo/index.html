How Technical Problems Affect Search Visibility

Before a page can rank, Google has to discover it, crawl it, render it and decide to index it. A problem at any step stops the page there. A stray noindex tag hides a page completely. A canonical tag pointing elsewhere tells Google to rank a different URL. Broken links and long redirect chains waste crawling and frustrate visitors.

Speed matters too. Google measures real-user experience with Core Web Vitals, and its guidance sets “good” at a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less, an Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less and a Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less.

These problems are usually invisible to the business owner, which is why they can hold a site back for months without anyone noticing.

Technical SEO Services Questions

What Is the Difference Between Technical SEO and On-Page SEO?

Technical SEO makes the site crawlable, indexable and fast. On-page SEO improves the content and HTML of individual pages, such as titles, headings and copy. Most sites need both.

Will Better PageSpeed Scores Improve My Rankings?

Speed is one signal among many. Faster pages help visitors and support rankings, but a perfect score will not outrank more relevant content on its own.

Can You Fix Technical SEO on Shopify or GoHighLevel?

Yes. I work on WordPress, Shopify and GoHighLevel, and on hand-coded sites like this one. Each platform has its own limits, which I explain up front.

How Do I Know If My Site Has Technical SEO Issues?

Search Console's Page Indexing report is the best starting point. You can also run my free SEO checker for a quick scan of the most common problems.

Do You Work With Developers?

Yes. I can implement fixes directly or write clear tickets for your developer and check the work afterwards.
