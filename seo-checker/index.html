Reading the page...

The check reads the page the way a crawler does. Some sites block automated tools, and Google's speed test can take up to a minute.

What the Checker Tests

Titles and Meta

Whether the title and meta description exist and fit Google's display limits (30 to 60 and 70 to 160 characters), and whether social preview tags are set.

Headings

That there is exactly one H1, enough H2 sections, and no skipped levels. You also get a full outline of every heading on the page.

Content

Word count (under 300 is thin), images missing alt text, and how many internal links point to your other pages.

Indexing

HTTP status, redirects, HTTPS, noindex tags and headers, canonical tag, mobile viewport, language, structured data, robots.txt and sitemap.

Speed

Google's own PageSpeed test on mobile: performance score, SEO score, Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift and Total Blocking Time.

Your Score

80 and above is healthy, 55 to 79 needs work, and below 55 has issues likely holding the page back. Problems are listed worst first.

Questions About the SEO Checker

Is the SEO Checker Free?

Yes. It's free to use with no sign-up. Check as many pages as you like.

What Does the SEO Checker Test?

It reads the live page and checks the title, meta description, social tags, headings, word count, image alt text, internal links, HTTP status, HTTPS, noindex tags, canonical tag, mobile viewport, language, structured data, robots.txt and sitemap. It also runs Google's PageSpeed test for speed and Core Web Vitals.

Why Does It Say a Site Blocked the Reader?

Some websites block automated tools. When that happens, the checker uses Google's Lighthouse audits instead, so you still get a report, but word count and the heading outline aren't available.

Does It Check My Whole Website?

No. It checks one page at a time. A full audit crawls every page and covers far more, including duplicate content, redirects and internal linking across the site.

Do You Store the URLs I Check?

No. This site doesn't save the addresses you check or the results.
