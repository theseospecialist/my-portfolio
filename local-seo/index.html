What Local SEO Is and Why Your Business Needs It

When someone searches for a service with a place in mind, Google usually shows a map with three businesses above the regular results. Google says those local results are based mainly on three things: relevance (how well your profile matches the search), distance (how far you are from the searcher) and prominence (how well known and well reviewed you are).

You can't move your address, but you can improve relevance and prominence. That is what local SEO does: it makes your profile, website and listings clearly match what customers search, and builds the reviews and mentions that show Google you are a real, trusted business.

For a restaurant group I manage in Florida, Google's own year-over-year comparison shows +600.1% direction requests and +90.8% website clicks after the profiles were optimized and actively managed.

Local SEO Services Questions

How Long Does Local SEO Take to Work?

Profile and listing fixes can show movement within a few weeks. Steady growth in calls and direction requests usually builds over three to six months, depending on competition and how many reviews you earn.

Can You Guarantee a Top 3 Map Ranking?

No, and nobody honest can. Distance from the searcher is a ranking factor no one controls. What I can do is improve relevance and prominence, then report the calls and visits that result.

Do I Need a Website for Local SEO?

A Business Profile can rank without one, but a fast website with service and location pages makes the profile stronger and gives you a second way to appear in search.

Do You Work With Multi-Location Businesses?

Yes. I currently manage profiles for multi-location restaurant and coffee brands, each location with its own profile, posts and reporting.

What Is NAP Consistency and Why Does It Matter?

NAP stands for name, address and phone number. When those details match everywhere, Google can be confident the listings belong to the same business, which supports your prominence.
