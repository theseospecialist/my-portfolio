What Makes a Page Easy for AI to Cite?

AI answers draw on pages they can reach, read without JavaScript and quote cleanly. The checker scores seven areas.

robots.txt

AI Crawler Access

Whether each AI crawler is allowed to fetch the page, using the same matching rules crawlers use, plus a live test for firewall and CDN blocks.

noindex, nosnippet

Snippet Controls

Noindex keeps a page out of search entirely, and nosnippet keeps it out of Google AI Overviews and featured snippets.

How much of your content exists in the HTML before any script runs, because most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript.

Questions About AI Visibility

What Is an AI Visibility Checker?

An AI visibility checker tests whether AI search tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can reach, read and quote a web page. This one checks robots.txt rules for 13 AI crawlers, firewall blocks, snippet controls, content that needs JavaScript, answer-first structure, schema and llms.txt.

How Can I Tell If ChatGPT Can See My Website?

ChatGPT search finds pages through OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot crawler. Run the checker on your page: it reads your robots.txt rules for OAI-SearchBot and sends a test request identifying as it, then reports whether the page is allowed and whether the server answered normally.

Does Blocking GPTBot Remove My Site From ChatGPT?

No. GPTBot collects data for training future OpenAI models. ChatGPT search results come from OAI-SearchBot, a separate crawler, so a site can block GPTBot and still appear in ChatGPT search, as long as OAI-SearchBot is allowed.

Does Google Use llms.txt?

Google has said its search systems do not use llms.txt, and Google AI Overviews rely on the normal Googlebot crawl. llms.txt is a proposed standard that some AI tools and agents read, so the checker reports it as a low-priority item.

Why Does JavaScript Matter for AI Visibility?

Most AI crawlers, including GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, read the raw HTML of a page and do not run JavaScript. If your content only appears after JavaScript runs, those crawlers see an almost empty page, even when Google can render it.

Does a High Score Mean AI Tools Will Cite Me?

No. The score measures readiness: whether AI systems can reach, read and quote your page. Whether they cite you also depends on your authority, your competitors and the exact question asked, which a full GEO audit covers.
