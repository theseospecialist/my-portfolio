Questions About My SEO Services

It depends on the size of your site, your competition and how much work is needed. I review your site for free first, then quote a fixed price for a one-off project or a monthly retainer, so you know the cost before any work starts.

Both. Technical SEO audits, website builds and content packages work well as one-off projects. Ongoing SEO, local SEO and content programs work best monthly, because results compound over time.
