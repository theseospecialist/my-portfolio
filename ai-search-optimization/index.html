How AI-Powered Search Experiences Work

AI search tools write an answer, then show links to the sources they drew from. Google's AI Overviews appear at the top of many results pages and draw on pages from Google's index. ChatGPT search and other assistants retrieve pages from the web and cite them alongside the answer.

Google has said that the same SEO best practices apply to its AI features: pages need to be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet, and there is no special markup required to be included. That is why AI search optimization starts with SEO, then improves how clearly your content answers the questions people ask.

It is measurable. For a home improvement brand in Manhattan, Search Console showed 12.2K impressions inside AI Overviews in three months, led by blog content I planned and published.

AI Search Optimization Questions

What Is the Difference Between GEO and AEO?

AEO focuses on being the direct answer in features like featured snippets and voice results. GEO focuses on being cited inside AI-written answers such as AI Overviews and ChatGPT. Both rely on clear, well-structured content.

Do I Still Need SEO If I Want AI Visibility?

Yes. AI Overviews draw on pages from Google's index, and AI assistants cite pages they can find and trust. Strong SEO is the foundation AI visibility is built on.

Can You Guarantee My Business Will Appear in ChatGPT?

No. AI tools decide which sources to cite, and their answers vary. I improve the signals that make citation more likely and track the results honestly.

How Do You Measure AI Search Visibility?

I use Google Search Console data, including AI Overview performance where it is available, plus regular checks of how assistants answer questions about your services.

Do I Need an llms.txt File?

It is optional. An llms.txt file summarizes your site for AI tools, and it is quick to add, but it is not a ranking factor and no major search engine requires it.
