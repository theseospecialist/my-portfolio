What Is GEO, and Why Does It Matter Now?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, means making your business the source that AI tools like Google's AI Overviews and ChatGPT quote when they answer a question. AEO, answer engine optimization, is the same idea for direct answers and featured snippets.

  • Answer FirstEach page opens with a clear, direct answer to the question people ask.
  • Structure That Machines ReadSchema, clean headings and FAQs, so AI tools can pull out the facts.
  • Consistent IdentityThe same name, services and details across your site, profiles and listings.
  • Track ItSearch Console's AI reporting shows when your pages appear in AI answers.

Questions People Ask

What Does an SEO Specialist Do?

An SEO specialist improves how often and how high your website appears in search results. That covers fixing technical problems, improving page content, earning backlinks, managing your Google Business Profile, and tracking the results in Search Console and analytics.

What Is the Difference Between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in regular search results. AEO, answer engine optimization, helps your content appear as direct answers and featured snippets. GEO, generative engine optimization, helps AI tools like Google's AI Overviews and ChatGPT cite your business in their answers.

How Long Does SEO Take to Show Results?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. In one project, organic clicks grew from 48 to 635 in the quarter after the audit fixes went live. Content and authority building usually take three to six months to compound.

Is the Free SEO Health Check Accurate?

It reads the live page and checks real on-page factors: the title, meta description, headings, word count, image alt text, canonical and noindex tags, schema, robots.txt and sitemap. Speed scores come straight from Google's PageSpeed Insights. It checks one page at a time, so a full audit covers far more.
