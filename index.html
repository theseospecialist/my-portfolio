Questions US Business Owners Ask

What Does a Freelance SEO Consultant Do?

A freelance SEO consultant finds what stops your website from ranking, fixes it and grows your traffic from there. That covers technical SEO, on-page optimization, content, link building, your Google Business Profile and reporting from Search Console and GA4. As a freelancer, you work with the person doing the work instead of an account manager.

Can an SEO Consultant in Pakistan Help a US Business Rank?

Yes. SEO depends on your website, your content and your Google data, not on where the consultant sits. My documented results include a marine retailer in Florida that went from 48 to 635 organic clicks in one quarter and a home improvement brand in Manhattan that reached 326K impressions from a standing start.

How Long Does SEO Take to Show Results?

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. In one project, organic clicks grew from 48 to 635 in the quarter after the audit fixes went live. Content and authority building usually take three to six months to compound.

What Is the Difference Between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in regular search results. AEO, answer engine optimization, helps your content appear as direct answers and featured snippets. GEO, generative engine optimization, helps AI tools like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity cite your business in their answers.

Do You Work With Local Businesses in My City?

Yes. Local SEO is remote work by nature: Google Business Profile optimization, local landing pages, citations and reviews. I currently manage Business Profiles for locations in Florida, and the same process works for any US city or service area.
