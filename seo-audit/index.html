What an SEO Audit Is, and What It Is Not

A good audit answers three questions: what is stopping search engines from ranking this site, where is the biggest untapped opportunity, and what should be done first. It is a diagnosis with a plan attached.

It is not a list of 300 automated warnings. Tools flag everything; most of it doesn't matter. My job is to separate the five issues that are costing you traffic from the hundred that aren't, and explain each one in plain English.

Not every recommendation produces a ranking change, and some take months to show up. I will tell you which fixes are likely to matter most, and which are housekeeping.

SEO Audit Services Questions

How Long Does an SEO Audit Take?

Most audits for small and medium business sites take one to two weeks, depending on the number of pages and whether Search Console and Analytics access is available.

Will an SEO Audit Improve My Rankings?

The audit itself changes nothing on your site. It identifies the problems and opportunities. Rankings improve when the fixes are made, and some changes take months to show.

Do You Need Access to Google Search Console?

It isn't required, but it makes the audit far more accurate, because Search Console shows exactly how Google crawls, indexes and ranks your pages.

Can You Implement the Fixes After the Audit?

Yes. For the marine retailer above, I delivered the audit and then implemented the fixes myself. You can also hand the plan to your own developer.

How Is This Different From Your Free SEO Checker?

The free checker scans one page for common problems in seconds. A full audit covers the whole site, your data, your competitors and a prioritized plan.
